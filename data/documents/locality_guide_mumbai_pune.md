# Locality Guide: Mumbai & Pune (Sample)

Disclaimer: This is a generic, illustrative guide written for demonstration purposes only. Figures are approximate and fictional; they should not be relied upon for actual investment or purchase decisions. Always verify current data with a licensed local broker or the relevant municipal authority.

## Mumbai

### Andheri
A well-connected western-suburb locality with strong metro and rail access. Popular with IT and media professionals. Mixed inventory of older housing societies and newer high-rises. Amenities are generally good, including malls, hospitals and international schools nearby.

### Bandra
One of Mumbai's most sought-after localities, known for sea-facing properties, restaurants and proximity to the Bandra-Worli Sea Link. Premium pricing; older buildings undergoing redevelopment are common, which can affect timelines for possession.

### Powai
Home to large IT campuses and premium gated communities around Powai Lake. Strong social infrastructure -- schools, malls, and hospitals -- and a relatively planned layout compared to older parts of the city.

### Thane
A fast-growing satellite city connected to Mumbai by rail and expressway. Offers larger floor plans and lower prices per square foot than South and Central Mumbai, popular with families seeking more space for the money.

### Borivali
A northern-suburb locality bordering Sanjay Gandhi National Park, offering relatively greener surroundings. Good rail connectivity along the western line; a mix of mid-market and premium housing.

## Pune

### Hinjewadi
Anchor of Pune's IT hub (Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park). High rental demand from IT employees. Traffic congestion during peak hours is a known concern; ongoing metro extension is expected to improve connectivity.

### Kothrud
An established, centrally located residential locality with good schools, markets and civic infrastructure. Popular with families due to its balance of connectivity and quieter residential character.

### Wakad
Adjacent to Hinjewadi, benefiting from IT-driven rental demand while offering comparatively newer housing stock and planned societies.

### Viman Nagar
Close to Pune Airport, with a mix of commercial and residential development, popular with young professionals and expatriates.

### Baner
A rapidly developing locality with new high-rise societies, malls and restaurants; well connected to the Mumbai-Pune Expressway.
