# Locality Guide: Bangalore & Hyderabad (Sample)

Disclaimer: This is a generic, illustrative guide for demonstration purposes only. Figures are approximate and fictional; verify with a licensed local broker before making decisions.

## Bangalore

### Whitefield
A major IT corridor with numerous tech parks (ITPL and others). Large inventory of apartment complexes ranging from mid-market to premium. Traffic on the main arterial roads can be heavy during commute hours; metro connectivity has been improving.

### Koramangala
A centrally located, mixed residential-commercial locality popular with startups and young professionals. High density of cafes, co-working spaces and social infrastructure; correspondingly higher per-square-foot pricing.

### Indiranagar
An established, leafy locality known for its restaurant and nightlife scene, along with a mature residential base. Metro connectivity along the Purple Line.

### HSR Layout
A well-planned layout with wide roads and greenery, popular with families and startup employees. Strong social infrastructure with parks, schools, and markets.

### Electronic City
An IT and electronics-manufacturing hub south of the city, connected via the Electronic City elevated expressway. Offers relatively affordable housing compared to the IT corridors closer to the city center.

## Hyderabad

### Gachibowli
Part of Hyderabad's HITEC City IT corridor, home to major technology campuses. High demand for both rental and ownership housing from IT professionals.

### Madhapur
Adjacent to Gachibowli, densely developed with apartment towers and commercial space; considered one of the pricier micro-markets in Hyderabad's west.

### Kondapur
A residential locality bridging the IT corridor and older parts of the city, offering a mix of independent houses and apartment complexes.

### Kukatpally
A well-established, more affordable locality with good metro connectivity via the Hyderabad Metro Green Line, popular with mid-income buyers.

### Banjara Hills
One of Hyderabad's most premium localities, known for upscale housing, boutiques and restaurants.
