# Amenities & Society Rules Brochure (Sample -- Generic Housing Society)

Disclaimer: This is a fictional, generic brochure created for demonstration purposes only and does not describe any real housing society.

## Amenities Overview -- "Sample Residency" (Illustrative)

- Clubhouse with indoor games room, gymnasium and multipurpose hall
- Swimming pool (with separate shallow pool for children)
- Landscaped gardens and children's play area
- 24x7 security with CCTV surveillance at all entry/exit points
- Power backup for common areas and essential in-flat load
- Reserved and visitor parking, with EV charging points in select blocks
- Rainwater harvesting and a dedicated sewage treatment plant (STP)
- Jogging track and open-air amphitheater
- Intercom and video door-phone connectivity to the main gate

## Society Rules (Illustrative Summary)

### Maintenance Charges
Monthly maintenance is typically billed per square foot of carpet area and covers common-area upkeep, security staff salaries, housekeeping, and a contribution to the sinking fund for major repairs. Charges and billing cycles vary by society; confirm current rates with the managing committee.

### Move-in / Move-out
Residents moving in or out are generally required to inform the society office in advance and may need to pay a refundable move-in/move-out deposit to cover potential damage to common areas (lifts, lobbies).

### Pets
Most societies permit pets subject to registration with the society office and adherence to common-area leash rules; specific breed or size restrictions vary by society and should be confirmed directly.

### Renovation / Interior Work
Structural changes typically require prior written approval from the society's managing committee and, where applicable, the municipal authority. Non-structural interior work is usually permitted within specified working hours to minimize disturbance to neighbors.

### Visitor and Vehicle Policy
Visitors are typically required to register at the security gate. Vehicle parking is usually allotted per unit, with additional vehicles subject to availability and, at some societies, an additional monthly charge.

### Noise and Common-Area Conduct
Quiet hours (commonly late evening to early morning) are typically observed for construction and loud activities. Use of clubhouse and amenity spaces is usually subject to booking slots managed by the society office, especially for events.

Note: Actual rules, timings, and charges differ significantly by society/builder and are governed by the society's registered bye-laws (often based on Model Bye-Laws under the applicable state Co-operative Societies Act) or the Apartment Owners' Association's own regulations. Always request the current bye-laws document from the society before purchase.
