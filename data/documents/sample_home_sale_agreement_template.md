# Sample Home Sale Agreement (Generic Template)

Disclaimer: This is a generic, fictional template provided for illustrative purposes only. It does not constitute legal advice and must not be used as-is for an actual transaction. Consult a qualified property lawyer and have any agreement reviewed and registered as required under applicable state law before signing.

## Agreement for Sale of Immovable Property

This Agreement for Sale ("Agreement") is made on [DATE] between:

**Seller**: [Seller Name], residing at [Seller Address] ("Seller")

**Buyer**: [Buyer Name], residing at [Buyer Address] ("Buyer")

### 1. Property Description
The Seller agrees to sell and the Buyer agrees to purchase the residential property described as:
- Flat/Unit No.: [Unit Number]
- Building/Society: [Building Name]
- Locality: [Locality], [City]
- Carpet Area: [Area] sq. ft.
- Survey/Plot No.: [Survey Number]

### 2. Sale Consideration
The total sale consideration agreed between the parties is Rs. [Amount] ("Sale Price"), payable as follows:
- Token/Booking Amount: Rs. [Amount] paid on signing this Agreement.
- Balance amount: Rs. [Amount] payable at or before execution of the Sale Deed, or in installments as agreed in Schedule A.

### 3. Title and Encumbrances
The Seller represents that the property is free from all encumbrances, mortgages, liens, and litigation, and that the Seller has clear and marketable title. The Buyer is advised to conduct independent title verification (see the accompanying Buyer's Legal Checklist document) before making any payment.

### 4. Possession
The Seller shall hand over vacant and peaceful possession of the property to the Buyer on or before [Possession Date], subject to full payment of the Sale Price.

### 5. Stamp Duty and Registration
Stamp duty and registration charges shall be borne by the Buyer, in accordance with the rates prevailing in [State] at the time of registration.

### 6. RERA Disclosure
Where applicable under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 ("RERA"), the Seller/promoter shall provide the project's RERA registration number and disclose the same to the Buyer.

### 7. Default and Termination
In the event of default by either party, the aggrieved party shall be entitled to remedies as provided under applicable law, including specific performance or refund with agreed compensation, as detailed in Schedule B.

### 8. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the applicable state property laws.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first written above.

Seller Signature: _______________   Buyer Signature: _______________

Witness 1: _______________   Witness 2: _______________

---
Note: This template omits Schedules A and B and numerous clauses (indemnity, force majeure, dispute resolution/arbitration, GST applicability for under-construction property, etc.) that a real agreement would require. Use only as a discussion starting point with your lawyer.
